Happenings — Page Templates & Wireframes

Three modular wireframes for events on cfbhall.com: two detail-page templates (Full-Service / Transactional for high-impact ticketed events, and Lead Generation & RSVP for non-ticketed / community events) plus a denser Happenings listing. Every situational component — Facebook Event embed, sticky CTA, and the location/share sidebar — is designed as an optional, admin-toggleable block.

  • Chrome (live-matching rail · hours bar · topbar · footer)
  • Optional block — site admin can enable / disable
  • Layout toggle — full-width vs. location/share sidebar
1 · Full-Service / Transactional 2 · Lead Gen & RSVP 3 · Happenings Listing
01

Full-Service / Transactional Detail

Target event type: high-impact, ticketed events (game-day parties, premium & fundraising programs, workshops). Goal: move the visitor to purchase fast — above the fold.

Key requirements: prominent ticket-purchasing integration (single Ventrata product widget), optional sticky CTA that follows on scroll, full-width content option (removes the default location/share sidebar), optional Facebook Event embed, and a compact related-events carousel.

02

Lead Generation & RSVP Detail

Target event type: non-ticketed, informational, or community-centric events (panels, film screenings, free community days, charity drives). Goal: capture sign-ups and RSVPs plus email newsletter sign-ups.

Key requirements: above-the-fold engagement (direct RSVP / sign-up + dedicated form fields and newsletter capture), optional sticky CTA, full-width content option, optional Facebook Event embed, and a compact related-events carousel.

03

Happenings — Listing Page

Target: the main Happenings listing. Requirement: optimize / reduce the size of event cards so more fit per row and the page scrolls less. No location/share sidebar.

Cards show only the essentials (compact image, date, title, small category tag) — excerpts are trimmed and icons reduced. Filter chips let visitors narrow by type (Upcoming · Free/RSVP · Ticketed · Exhibitions) and keep the grid dense.
